Lucas FM starter collection: Park in D, prepared for D / 72 BPM.

Birds, River, Wind, Birdsong, Stream, Park air: excerpts from Park Ambiences
by Thimras, CC0 1.0, published 2022-07-25.
https://opengameart.org/content/park-ambiences
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Original files: park_ambience_birds.wav, park_ambience_river.wav,
park_ambience_wind.wav. Original hashes and excerpt offsets: provenance.json.
Converted to mono / 16 kHz; trimmed, normalized and boundary tapered by lucas_pack.py.

Warm pad, Glass pad, Sine pad: rendered from SLOOP's ANALOG WARM PAD,
DIGITAL GLASS PAD and ANALOG SINE PAD at D4. SLOOP / Felucca code credits
and licenses remain in LICENSING.md. Reproduce with tests/lucas_render.c.
Soft beat: original one-bar 72 BPM arrangement of Versilian Studios VCSL
CC0 kick, snare and hi-hat. See ../samples-cc0/ATTRIBUTION.txt for exact sources.
The original arrangement and generated pad recordings are dedicated to CC0.

Personal Desktop samples are prepared separately and are not published.
Storage is shared with SLOOP user sample instruments and drum kits. Installing
this collection replaces all four user sample slots; export a backup first.
