About this instrument

Lucas Loops 0.4 runs the real FM-1 firmware in your browser at 240 MHz. Ten loops in D major at 72 BPM. Save while stopped to keep your setup here. No physical device is connected.

Emulator by fm1-emu (MIT). Firmware based on SLOOP / Felucca (GPL-3.0). Nunito font (SIL OFL).
